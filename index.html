Member · Iron block

How a block works

Three moves. Then repeat.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Your intro is a conversation and a movement screen. We set a starting load before we set a goal.

  2. 02

    Build

    Classes follow a block, not a random whiteboard. Strength, engine, and skill each have a lane.

  3. 03

    Adjust

    Coaches change the plan when your life, sleep, or joints change. Progress is the point, not punishment.
