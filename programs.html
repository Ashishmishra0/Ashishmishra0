Programs

Six lanes. One standard.

Every class has a coach, a clock, and a way to scale. Mix the week or stay in one lane until the block is done.

One rep. Then set it down.

01

Iron

60 min · Strength · Platforms

Barbell strength for people who want the big lifts to look the same on rep five as they did on rep one. Squat, hinge, push, and pull rotate through the block.

  • Loads posted as ranges, not a single heroic number
  • Technique notes written on the board before the clock starts
  • A fundamentals option in the late morning for newer lifters

02

Engine

30–45 min · Conditioning · Deck

Intervals with a purpose: repeatable pieces that build a bigger engine and still leave your strength work intact.

  • Rower, ski, sled, and carry stations with a coach on the transitions
  • Short Engine 30 at dawn, longer pieces in the evening
  • Pacing targets so the first round is not the whole session

03

Ringcraft

50 min · Boxing · Ring

Guard, feet, and bag rounds. The class teaches the skill, then lets the sweat catch up. No sparring in the group hour.

  • Gloves and wraps are available at the desk
  • Partner drills stay technical — power is optional
  • Open skill slots for members who want extra rounds

04

Yard

55 min · Mixed · Deck

A coached mixed session for people training toward a race, a test, or just a harder Saturday. Stations rotate. The plan does not.

  • Run, carry, sled, and a strength piece in one hour
  • Scaled distances posted beside the full effort
  • Built to sit next to Iron, not replace it

05

Reset

40 min · Mobility · Quiet bay

Hips, shoulders, breath, and positions you actually use under a bar. This is training, not a cooldown you skip.

  • Low lights, no leaderboard
  • Useful if you lift heavy or sit all day
  • Members on Elite can book the bay outside class

06

Private

50 min · One to one · Any room

Coaching for a specific lift, a return from time off, or a block you want watched closely. Elite membership includes two sessions a month.

  • Booked with a named coach
  • Plan written down so the next class matches it
  • Not a sales hour — you train
